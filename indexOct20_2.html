About South Africa Business Hub

What we are

South Africa Business Hub is a local business directory built to help people discover, support and shop local. We bring businesses from every province together in one easy-to-use place, so customers can find them and communities can grow stronger.

Why we can help your business

  • Get found: be listed where customers are already searching for local products and services.
  • Province-focused reach: your listing sits in your own province’s directory, in front of people nearby.
  • Simple to join: register in minutes through WhatsApp, with real people to guide you.
  • Stronger together: a growing network of local businesses supporting one another.
  • Mobile-first: built to work quickly on any phone, even on slower connections.

Frequently asked questions

What is the South Africa Business Hub?

It’s an online directory of local South African businesses, organised by province, making it easy for people to find and support them.

How do I get my business listed?

Tap “Register” in the “Get your business listed” section. It opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message, so you only need to press send and we’ll get you started.

Which provinces do you cover?

All nine: Gauteng, KwaZulu Natal, Western Cape, North West, Eastern Cape, Mpumalanga, Free State, Limpopo and Northern Cape.

How do customers find me?

Customers choose their province and browse the businesses listed there, so your listing is seen by people looking locally.

What information do I need to register?

Just the basics: your business name, what you offer, your location and how customers can contact you. We’ll guide you through the rest on WhatsApp.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Use the WhatsApp tab at the bottom of the screen and our team will respond as soon as they can.
