About South Africa Business Hub

What we are

South Africa Business Hub is a local business directory built to help people discover, support and shop local. We bring local businesses together in one easy-to-use place, so customers can find them and communities can grow stronger.

Why we can help your business

  • Get found: be listed where customers are already searching for local products and services.
  • Local reach: your listing helps people nearby discover your products and services.
  • Simple to join: register in minutes through WhatsApp, with real people to guide you.
  • Stronger together: a growing network of local businesses supporting one another.
  • Mobile-first: built to work quickly on any phone, even on slower connections.

Frequently asked questions

What is the South Africa Business Hub?

It’s an online directory of local South African businesses, making it easy for people to find and support them.

How do I get my business listed?

Tap “Register”. It opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message, so you only need to press send and we’ll get you started.

How do customers find me?

Customers browse categories and discover businesses that match what they need, helping your listing reach people looking locally.

What information do I need to register?

Just the basics: your business name, what you offer, your location and how customers can contact you. We’ll guide you through the rest on WhatsApp.

Who do I contact if I need help?

Use the WhatsApp tab at the bottom of the screen and our team will respond as soon as they can.
